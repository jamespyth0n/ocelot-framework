🎬 The Interview

A comedy in acts. Based on real events. Names unchanged because nobody would believe them anyway.

🎬 Act I — The Resume

"So how did you build this?" — "I blabbered, made a bad draft, and let the guys do the rest."

🔥 Act II — The Architecture

"You ran a 14-billion-parameter model with no dedicated GPU?" — "I know what a boiled egg is."

🧪 Act III — The Boiled Egg

"Show us the receipts." — Campaign 004, five questions, three voices, blind evaluation. The egg boils.

📚 Act IV — The Evidence

"Where does the knowledge live?" — 1,332 files. 45 folders. One index. The Library of Alexandria.

💀 Act V — The Identical Twins

"Change this to this." — "...they're the same." — "No, the BRACKET." — "You know what, I go for vape."

🪨 Act VI — The Philosophy

"Did you just quote the Bible in a technical interview?" — "I quoted the truth. The Bible just got there first."

🚪 Act VII — The Closer

"Any last words?" — "You can always buy more hardware. You can't buy correctness." *drops mic, exits*
