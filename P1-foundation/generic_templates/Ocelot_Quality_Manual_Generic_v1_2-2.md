## 7. Support

### 7.1 Resources

#### 7.1.1 General

| Resource | Description | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Human Partner** | Framework Architect & Quality Authority | Adapts to your schedule and availability |
| **AI Partner** | Ocelot 🐆 — AI Development Partner | Stateless per session — memoir engine compensates |
| **Platform** | Any LLM-compatible platform | Model-agnostic — GPT, Claude, Gemini, LLaMA, etc. |
| **Compute** | Developer's local machine | No GPU required — runs on consumer hardware |
| **Storage** | Persistent repository (Git, Cloud, or local) | Single source of truth for all artifacts |
| **Version Control** | Git or equivalent | Semantic versioning enforced |

> 💡 **Tip:** When you hit platform limits, don't panic — pivot to manual methods. The discipline is the same whether automated or manual.

#### 7.1.2 People

Two-person team with complementary strengths:

- **Human strengths:** Domain expertise, strategic vision, creative ideation, emotional intelligence, quality authority
- **AI strengths:** Code generation, pattern recognition, audit execution, documentation, tireless consistency

> 💡 **Tip:** The best partnerships leverage asymmetric strengths. Don't ask the AI to do what the human does better (strategic vision), and don't ask the human to do what the AI does better (line-by-line audits).
