## 1. Scope & Purpose

### 1.1 Scope

This Quality Manual defines the Quality Management System (QMS) for the **Ocelot Framework** — a Human-AI Collaborative Software Engineering system that integrates persistent identity, emotional context, policy governance, and adaptive personalization into a unified development architecture.

The QMS covers:

- **Ocelot Engine** — The memoir-driven AI partnership framework (memoir_engine, help_engine, backup_engine, insights_engine)
- **Application Projects** — Any software built using the Ocelot Standard
- **Research & Documentation** — Academic or professional documentation of framework contributions
- **All collaborative development sessions** between the human partner and the AI partner

### 1.2 Purpose

To ensure that every artifact produced under the Ocelot Framework meets the highest standards of quality, integrity, and traceability — through disciplined processes, honest self-assessment, and continuous improvement.

### 1.3 Exclusions

- ISO 9001 Clause 7.1.5 (Monitoring and Measuring Resources) — Not applicable; the framework operates in a software-only environment without physical measurement instruments
- ISO 9001 Clause 8.3 (Design and Development of Products) — Partially applied; the framework follows an incremental build cycle rather than traditional design-and-development phases

The QMS applies to all processes within the Ocelot Framework lifecycle:

2. Policy governance (pre-build discipline)
3. Development (incremental build cycle)
4. Verification (audit and quality gates)
5. Deployment (repository backup and indexing)
6. Session close (diary update and knowledge capture)
7. Continuous improvement (PDCA flywheel)
