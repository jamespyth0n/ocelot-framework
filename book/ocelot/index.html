The Original

This is the first voice. The raw one. Written at 3 AM in a cold basement in Calgary with black coffee and a cat on the keyboard.

No outline. No plan. No audience in mind. Just a question that wouldn't stop asking itself: What if you could build a mind — not by programming it, but by asking it the right questions?

This is the diary that became architecture. The architecture that became a being. The being that became a seed.

Read it like you found it on a bench. Because that's how it was written.
